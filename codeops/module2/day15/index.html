Our Story

we are ethiopia's first online food delivery service, bringing the rich flavors of Ethiopian cuisine to your doorstep. Our mission is to make authentic Ethiopian dishes accessible to everyone, whether you're craving a hearty Doro Wat or a fresh Veggie Combo.
We partner with local restaurants to ensure that every meal is prepared with the utmost care and quality. From traditional recipes passed down through generations to modern twists on classic dishes, we offer a diverse menu that caters to all tastes. Our easy-to-use platform allows you to browse menus, place orders, and track deliveries in real-time, making your dining experience seamless and enjoyable.
